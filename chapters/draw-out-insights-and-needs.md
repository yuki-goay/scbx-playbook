# Draw out insights and needs

You have hours of conversation transcripts and a head full of half-formed impressions. This capability turns all of that into something the whole team can design from: who the users are, what they think and feel, what they need and what they are trying to get done.

You start with a reviewed evidence log, the pain point register and your research plan. You finish with a persona set, empathy maps, a user need register and a jobs-to-be-done matrix. Each line points back to something a real person said or did.

---

## Key artefacts

| Activity | UX artefact | When it is worth doing |
|---|---|---|
| Decide how many personas you need and what sets them apart. Group participants, draft one-page profiles, then check them with people who know the users | User persona set | You have a large and varied user base whom you are unsure about |
| Decide what each empathy map covers. Place what you saw and heard, then challenge each other on what users think and feel | Empathy map | Right after an observation session, or before a big decision when the team does not agree on who the user is |
| Look across the maps for gaps between what users say and what they do, then name the pains and gains | Pains, gains and contradictions list | You have seen behaviour as well as heard answers |
| Turn pain points into need statements written from the user's side. Merge overlaps and give each need a stable ID | User need register | You have pain points and the team needs one shared list to design against |
| Pull out what users are trying to get done, in their words. Write job statements, group them, then rate them against each persona | Jobs-to-be-done matrix | You have to choose which problems to solve first |

---

## What can agents do?

**AG-02 Research Synthesis** groups participants into segments, drafts persona profiles and empathy maps, names pains and gains, assigns need IDs, and drafts job statements and the matrix. It cites an evidence item ID on every line.

**AG-01 Evidence Librarian** logs the need register entries with source links and confidence levels.

Neither agent chooses the scope, settles disagreements, sets the final wording of a need, or invents quotes or participants.

---

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> **Keep one list of pain points.** Insights derived from any research session all go into a consolidated list of pain points. This becomes the source of truth that populate your personas, needs, and journey down the road. Your decisions will always be evidence-backed so you know you are on the right track.
