# Test concepts

How do you know if your team's judgment is right? This capability puts them in front of real users before you commit to detailed design, so the decision to go forward rests on user response, not team opinion.

---

## Key artefacts

| Activity                                                                                                                                                                     | UX artefact                | When it is worth doing                                                              |
| ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Write the test plan: what you are testing, who you are testing with, how many sessions, what counts as a positive signal, and who makes the go-forward decision              | Test plan                  | Always, before prototype work begins                                                |
| Create lo-fi prototypes for each concept — sketches, paper mockups or very rough wireframes. Enough to communicate the idea, not enough to be mistaken for a finished design | Lo-fi prototypes           | You have more than one concept and participants need something concrete to react to |
| Run concept testing sessions with participants and take notes on what they say and do                                                                                        | Session notes              | You have an agreed test plan and at least two available participants per concept    |
| Synthesise the findings: what the feedback means for each concept, what the team would change before proceeding                                                              | Concept test findings      | Always, before the go-forward decision                                              |
| Make and record the go-forward decision: which concepts go forward, which are changed, which are dropped, and why                                                            | Go-forward decision record | Always, at the end of this capability                                               |

---

## What can agents do?

**AG-03 Research Ops** drafts the test plan from the agreed brief, and books sessions and distributes preparation materials once approved.

**AG-02 Research Synthesis** tags and clusters the concept test feedback by theme, and drafts the findings summary with evidence IDs and verbatim quotes by participant ID.

**AG-01 Evidence Librarian** logs the go-forward decision with its rationale and the evidence behind it.

None of these agents decides what goes forward, sets or revises success criteria after sessions end, or smooths over contradictions in the feedback.

---

> 💡 **Tips**
>
> **Concept testing is not usability testing.** You are testing whether the idea makes sense to users, not whether the design is easy to navigate.
>
