# Align and plan

When discussing about the project strategy, different people in the room may mean different things when they talk about it. This capability turns that fuzzy starting point into a clear, agreed basis that the whole team can work from.

You start with a brief or problem statement, any stakeholder input, and whatever prior research exists. You finish with a goal and scope statement, an assumptions and gaps register, and a research plan. Each output reflects what the team actually agreed, not what the original brief assumed.

---

## Key artefacts

| Activity                                                                                                                               | UX artefact                   | When it is worth doing                                                              |
| -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | ----------------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Agree the goal and scope with stakeholders: what the research will cover, what it will not, why it is needed now, and who has sign-off | Goal and scope statement      | Every time. Scope without sign-off is not scope                                     |
| Surface and list assumptions: what the team believes but has not tested, and what would change direction if it turned out to be wrong  | Assumptions and gaps register | Every time. Hidden assumptions drive decisions as much as evidence does             |
| Write the research plan: aims, methods, participants, timeline, sign-off requirements                                                  | Research plan                 | When you are about to run sessions with real people, or spend time on desk research |


---

## What can agents do?

**AG-02 Research Synthesis** can draft the assumptions list from the brief and prior research, citing a source for each entry, and surface scope items implied by the inputs.

**AG-03 Research Ops** can draft the research plan structure from the agreed brief, and prepare scheduling templates once the plan is approved.

Neither agent sets the scope, decides what is in or out, or sends anything before the plan is signed off.

---

> 💡 **Tip**
>
> **Scope creep starts here.** Adding every item discussed without proper rationale or validated assumption bloats the project scope. Keep it tight enough that you can actually deliver.
>
