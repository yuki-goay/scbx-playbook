# Test usability

Concept testing lets you know if you are designing the right thing, usability testing lets you know if you are designing the thing right. You need to know whether your design holds up: against the principles you agreed, and against real users trying to complete real tasks. This is the final quality gate before handoff.

---

## Key artefacts

| Activity                                                                                                                 | UX artefact                 | When it is worth doing                                                             |
| ------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------ | --------------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Check the design against the accessibility standards and flag discrepancies                                              | Accessibility check results | Always, before usability testing begins                                            |
| Prepare the usability test: agree the tasks, the success criteria and what counts as a blocker before any session begins | Usability test plan         | When you have a prototype with at least one complete user journey                  |
| Run usability testing sessions with participants and observe where they succeed, hesitate or fail                        | Usability test observations | Always when participant access is available before handoff                         |
| Administer the SUS questionnaire after each session and collect the scores                                               | SUS scores                  | When you are running usability sessions and want a comparable metric across rounds |
| Synthesise findings: label each issue as a blocker, a wishlist item or a backlog item, with a reason for the category    | Usability findings report   | After every round of usability testing                                             |
| Agree what is complete enough to hand off, and record what was parked and why                                            | Handoff readiness decision  | Always, before handoff begins                                                      |

---

## What can agents do?

**AG-02 Research Synthesis** runs the principle check against the agreed experience principles and accessibility standards, and flags discrepancies with a reference to the principle or standard each one fails.

**AG-03 Research Ops** drafts the usability test plan and materials, books sessions and distributes materials once approved.

**AG-01 Evidence Librarian** logs principle check results, usability findings and SUS scores, and assembles findings by session and category.

None of these agents decides whether to fix or accept a discrepancy, sets the pass criteria, or categorises an issue as a blocker or wishlist item.

---

> 💡 **Tips**
>
> **Not every problem is a blocker.** A blocker stops a user completing a core task. Use human judgement to prioritise feedback given from users instead of acting on every single feedback given.
