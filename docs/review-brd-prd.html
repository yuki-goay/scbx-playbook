What are you reviewing?

The BRD explains why the initiative exists and what outcomes it must deliver. The PRD explains what the product must do and how anyone will know it is done. A BRD that starts choosing frameworks has wandered into PRD territory, and a PRD that cannot explain its business purpose has lost its BRD. Technical decisions only belong in a BRD when they are confirmed business constraints.

Who should be in the room?

Business owners, product leadership, architecture, security, governance and delivery stakeholders review the BRD. The PRD widens to include business analysts, engineering leads, test leads and operations, because they will all have to live with it.

When the output is weak

Resist the urge to fix it by hand. Give the agent your corrections and ask it to revise, so the document keeps its traceability and the correction becomes part of the record. If the same gap keeps appearing, the problem is probably upstream, in missing or conflicting source material, and that is a context fix rather than a prompting fix.

Handoff

An approved BRD goes to the PRD Builder, and an approved PRD goes to backlog creation and specification. Start a new chat session before invoking each next agent, so the new phase begins from the approved document and not from the leftovers of the last conversation.
