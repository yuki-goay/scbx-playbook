Review a spec or plan

What are you reviewing?

The spec says what must be built and how anyone will know it works. The plan says how, and the tasks break the plan into ordered, bounded pieces of work. They are reviewed by different people at different gates, but the habits are the same, so they share a page. The Product Owner approves the spec, the FDA approves the plan, and the FDE approves the tasks.

When the output is weak

Give the agent your corrections and ask it to revise, rather than editing by hand, so traceability survives. If a clarification needs a business or technical answer, route it to the accountable role. The FDE coordinates, but the Product Owner and FDA own the answers within their areas. If the same gap keeps coming back, treat it as a context problem and fix the source document the agent is reading from.

Handoff

The next stage starts manually, after you have read the agent's NEXT_STEP status. Spec goes to the Implementation Planner, and the approved plan and tasks go to the Implementation Builder. Start a new chat session for each, using the same RUN_ID, so every stage begins from approved artefacts and not from a long conversation of half remembered decisions.
