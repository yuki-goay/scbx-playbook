As a Product Owner,

you decide what is worth building, and whether it was built right.

What you are responsible for

As Product Owner you hold the vision, the roadmap, the backlog and the business outcomes, and you make the trade offs that shape them, from what earns a place in the MVP to what waits and what gets cut. Nothing moves forward until you have said that this is what the squad meant. Agents execute the work, but you answer for whether the value showed up once it shipped.

Your key decision points

Specify approval → Does this spec describe the thing we actually want, or just a confident guess at it?
Requirements and backlog sign off → Are the priorities, scope and acceptance criteria right?
Deployment approval (with PM) → Are we ready to put this in front of real users?
Reuse or regenerate (brownfield) → Is the existing approved BRD or PRD still good enough, or has it gone stale?

What you review

BRD
Are the right stakeholders named?
Are contradictions between them resolved rather than smoothed over?
Are objectives quantified?
Are scope boundaries clear?

PRD
Is every story tagged P0, P1 or P2?
Is the MVP boundary obvious?
Are technical constraints and non functional requirements present?

Backlog
Do the epics and stories reflect your priorities?
Does each story have Given/When/Then acceptance criteria?
Is each story linked to a parent epic?

spec.md
Does it trace back to your requirements?
Were open questions answered rather than quietly turned into assumptions?

These are the agents you'll most likely work with:

Research Planner

Turns your vision and stakeholder notes into an assumptions log, a research plan, interview guides and screeners.

BRD Builder

Converts business problems, objectives and discovery inputs into a structured and traceable Business Requirements Document.

PRD Builder

Translates approved business direction into detailed, measurable and testable product requirements for downstream design, engineering and validation.

Jira Backlog Manager

Transforms approved product requirements or epics into a backlog of epics, user stories and acceptance criteria.
