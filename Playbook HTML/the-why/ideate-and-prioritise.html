Proposal Ideate and prioritise

This capability takes you into the first wave of solution thinking: generating ideas, grounding them in what users actually need, and making a first round of decisions about what is worth pursuing.

You work on prioritising a high-level feature set, a to-be service blueprint showing what the improved experience looks like, and a short set of design principles to ground your solutions. Each idea in the feature set traces back to a user need or job.

Key artefacts

Activity UX artefact When it is worth doing
For each underserved job in the matrix, generate what the product could do. Keep generation and evaluation in separate sessions Idea list You have a jobs-to-be-done matrix and a team ready to build on it
Sketch the to-be service blueprint: what the improved experience looks like when the prioritised ideas are in place To-be service blueprint You need to show what is actually different from today before committing to detailed design
Draft design principles for this product: short statements about how the experience should feel and behave, written so each one could reject a design Design principles The team needs a shared yardstick for judging designs before screens start to multiply
Decide which ideas go forward, which are parked and which are out of scope. Record the reason for each High-level feature set Always, before concept testing or detailed design begins

What can agents do?

AG-02 Research Synthesis tags each idea with the job and user need it addresses, flags any idea with no evidence basis, proposes clusters and identifies duplicates, and lists each feature candidate with its evidence.

AG-04 Design Organiser drafts the to-be service blueprint structure from the as-is blueprint and agreed clusters, and formats the high-level feature set with in, parked and out-of-scope columns.

AG-01 Evidence Librarian logs the prioritisation decisions and the rationale behind each one.

💡 Tips

Think big and expansive. In traditional design thinking fashion, diverge before converging. The sky's the limit to what your solutions could be. Do not be bogged down by feasibility at this point.
