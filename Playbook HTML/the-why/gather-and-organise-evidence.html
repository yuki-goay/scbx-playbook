Proposal Gather and organise evidence

Whiteboard sketches, meeting recordings and research notes are piling up. The pile grows faster than anyone can make sense of it. This capability turns that growing pile into an evidence base the whole team can trust and use.

You start with the signed-off research plan, interview guides, raw session notes and any desk research. You finish with a tagged, searchable evidence repository where every item traces to a source, and a pain point register where every entry has a participant and a confidence level.

Key artefacts

Activity UX artefact When it is worth doing
Conduct stakeholder interviews and exploratory research sessions per the plan Raw session notes and recordings When there are unvalidated gaps or when the team is not aligned
Process and tag cleared evidence: source, participant, session date, evidence type, topic, confidence level Tagged evidence log After each session, not in a single pass at the end
Edit and maintain the evidence repository ensuring it is structured, searchable, every item traceable to its source Evidence repository Every time you add a new batch of evidence
Extract and log pain points from the evidence, with participant attribution and confidence scores Pain point register Once enough evidence exists to see patterns forming

What can agents do?

AG-01 Evidence Librarian processes cleared transcripts and notes, tags each item by source, participant, session date, evidence type, topic and confidence level, builds and maintains the evidence repository, and extracts and logs pain points with participant attribution.

AG-03 Research Ops handles fieldwork logistics — booking, reminders and materials distribution — once approved.

Neither agent reads raw recordings or notes before a person has cleared them, or processes any item without citing its source.

💡 Tips

Confidence levels are honest work. "Heard once, unconfirmed" is more useful than treating everything as equally valid.

The repository is the team's shared memory. Keep it current. An out-of-date repository looks complete when it is not.
