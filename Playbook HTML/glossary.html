Glossary

Key terms, defined plainly.

ABCFGHMPSV

A

Agentic PDLC
Agentic PDLC shifts product delivery from a sequence of predominantly human-executed activities to a governed system in which people and agents work across the lifecycle.
Source: 1.1

Not yet defined

BRD Business Requirements Document
A structured and traceable document that converts business problems, objectives and discovery inputs into business requirements for stakeholder review and approval.
Source: Appendix A
Brownfield enhancement
Retain the existing application and deliver a feature, regulatory change or increment.
