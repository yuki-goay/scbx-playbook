5.3.1 Senior Engineering / Forward Deployed Architect: from expert implementer to context architect

SECTION SUMMARY Senior engineering judgement increasingly shapes the architecture, constraints and context through which the squad and agents deliver.

The value of senior engineering has always depended heavily on judgement. Agentic delivery makes that more explicit. Rather than spending the majority of time personally implementing the hardest code, senior engineering effort increasingly moves towards architecture, context engineering, review, escalation and mentoring.

Traditional emphasis
Agentic product squad
Primary contribution: writes or resolves the most complex and critical implementation work.
Primary contribution: shapes the architecture and context through which people and agents deliver.
Architecture: applies patterns and resolves difficult design decisions.
Architecture: defines the boundaries, patterns and constraints that agents and engineers must respect.
Knowledge: shares engineering judgement through experience, guidance and code review.
Context engineering: translates architectural knowledge into explicit instructions, conventions, configurations and reusable context.
Review: reviews code for quality, maintainability and architectural fit.
Review and escalation: evaluates agent output against architectural intent, identifies subtle violations and handles cases that agents cannot resolve.
Mentoring: teaches implementation techniques and engineering practices.
Mentoring: teaches engineers how to write effective specifications, evaluate agent output and develop engineering judgement.
Leverage: personally solves the hardest engineering problems.
Leverage: creates the conditions that allow the wider squad and agents to solve work consistently.

The shift is from “the person who writes the hardest code” towards “the person who shapes the system that writes the code.”

A central capability in this shift is context engineering: deciding what architectural knowledge, standards, conventions and constraints need to be made explicit so that agents can act consistently with engineering intent.

Within the target Product Squad, the exact mapping depends on scope. Responsibilities close to implementation may remain with a senior Application Engineer, while architecture-wide responsibilities align more closely to the Forward Deployed Architect (FDA).
