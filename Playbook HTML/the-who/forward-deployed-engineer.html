5.3.3 Forward Deployed Engineer: from Tech Lead to delivery orchestrator

SECTION SUMMARY The Tech Lead’s coordination role expands from allocating work across engineers to orchestrating work across people and agents.

The Tech Lead role shifts from primarily assigning tasks to people towards determining how work should be executed across humans and agents. This requires judgement about which tasks are appropriate for agent execution, which require human implementation and which are best handled through a hybrid approach.

Traditional emphasis
Agentic product squad
Work allocation: assigns work based on role, expertise and capacity.
Execution choice: determines whether work should be human-led, agent-executed or hybrid.
Decomposition: breaks stories into implementable engineering tasks.
Agent-aware decomposition: identifies where complex work can be broken into bounded, agent-suitable tasks.
Coordination: coordinates work and dependencies across engineers.
Orchestration: coordinates work across people and agents while ensuring the required context is available.
Problem resolution: intervenes when engineering work encounters difficult technical problems.
Escalation: recognises tasks or code paths that agents cannot handle reliably and routes them back to appropriate human judgement.
Continuous improvement: improves team practices through delivery experience and retrospectives.
Feedback-loop ownership: tracks recurring agent failures, identifies context gaps and prioritises improvements to the shared context and ways of working.

The role therefore requires more than deciding whether an apparently simple or complex task should go to an agent. A simple-looking task may involve code paths that agents consistently mishandle, while complex work may sometimes be decomposed into smaller tasks that agents can execute effectively.

Within the target Product Squad, these responsibilities align most closely with the Forward Deployed Engineer (FDE): bridging approved architecture and executable delivery, coordinating work across people and agents, and improving the context and delivery patterns that enable reliable execution.
